# Conversion Tracking Map

The pages include `data-track` attributes and push events into `window.dataLayer`.

## Primary conversions

Use these as Google Ads primary conversions after the phone number and lead handlers are live:

- `phone_intent`
- `sticky_phone_intent`
- `lead_form_submit`
- `chat_callback_click`

Recommended call conversion settings:

- Calls from ads: primary conversion once call assets are live.
- Website calls: primary conversion once Google forwarding number or call tracking provider is configured.
- Minimum call length: start with 60 seconds.

## Secondary conversions

Use these as secondary or observation events:

- `primary_cta_click`
- `prices_click`
- `sticky_prices_click`
- `form_button_click`
- `chat_toggle`
- `chat_quick_reply`
- `prices_contact_click`
